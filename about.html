About Student Life Hub

Student Life Hub is a small site written by a software engineering student currently finishing their degree while juggling coursework, projects, and everything else university throws at you.

The goal here is simple: write the advice on time management, study tools, and productivity that actually holds up when you're behind on three assignments at once — not generic listicle advice recycled from a hundred other sites.

This site also doubles as a hands-on SEO learning project — every page here is built and optimized deliberately as part of learning technical and on-page SEO in practice.
